Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Holdeman Concrete, Inc. ("Holdeman Concrete," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our website, contact us, request a quote or appointment, or call or text us. By using our website or contacting us, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, plus basic website usage data. This may include:

  • Contact information — your name, phone number, email address, and service (home or business) address.
  • Project or service details — information you share about the work you need, including photos or descriptions you send us.
  • Communications — the content of your calls, text messages, emails, and form submissions with us.
  • Website usage — basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected through cookies and tools like Google Analytics or advertising tags (see "Cookies & Analytics" below).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions and requests;
  • Prepare quotes and schedule your appointment or service;
  • Perform and follow up on the work you hire us for;
  • Contact you about your request by phone call, text message, or email;
  • Measure our advertising and improve our website and services.

Phone Calls & Text Messages (SMS)

When you provide your phone number — by filling out a form, booking online, calling us, or texting us — you agree that we may contact you by phone call and text message about your inquiry, quote, appointment, or service.

Your consent & how to opt out:

  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your request.
  • You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.
  • You can ask us to stop calling or texting at any time by telling us during a call or by contacting us using the details below.
  • Consent to receive calls or texts is not a condition of purchasing any product or service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or contact information with third parties for their own marketing.

Call Recording & Monitoring

We may record or monitor phone calls for quality assurance, training, and accurate record-keeping. If call recording is active, by continuing your call with us you consent to being recorded. If you do not wish to be recorded, please let us know at the start of the call or contact us another way, such as email or text.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only:

  • With trusted service providers who help us run our business — for example, our website form provider (Web3Forms); website analytics and advertising providers (such as Google) — and only as needed to provide those services;
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking, to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the site work.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may ask what information we have about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or opt out of calls and texts as described above. To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
